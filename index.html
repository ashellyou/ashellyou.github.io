Built for real work.

Four screens from the v8 pre-release. Pick one.

A terminal that knows ADB.

Continuous output for logcat and top, an Abort button that stops the process, and suggestions for commands, package names and permissions while you type.

  • Search, save and share output
  • Bookmarks and history
  • Errors print in their own colour

See it and touch it.

Mirror another device over USB or Wi-Fi and control it with multi-touch. Taps, swipes and pinches go straight through, and full screen keeps a floating control within reach.

  • Power, volume and screenshot keys
  • Immersive full screen
  • Built on scrcpy 4.1

Logs you can read.

Stream logcat in a foreground service that keeps going when you switch apps. Colour bars mark each level, and filter profiles stack: include one app, exclude the noisy ones.

  • Filter by package, tag, PID or TID
  • Pause, resume and search
  • This device or another one

Fastboot without the laptop.

Plug in a device in bootloader mode and see its codename, slot and lock state. Flash with a slide to confirm, read variables, wipe data, or drop to the console for raw commands.

  • Flash any partition
  • Wipe data, get variables
  • Reboot to any mode

And plenty more.

Quick Settings tiles

Up to ten tiles, each running a command through Shizuku or root. Try it: add a tile, then tap it.

folder_openFile browser

Push and pull over Wi-Fi or OTG with real ADB sync transfers. Multi-select, copy, cut, rename, and decide what happens on conflicts.

backupScheduled backups

Settings, commands, bookmarks and tiles, to an encrypted file or Google Drive. Daily, weekly or monthly.

What you need

Android 9 or newer. For commands on your own phone, use Shizuku, root or wireless debugging. For another device, your phone needs nothing extra: just enable USB or wireless debugging on the target.

Setup guides on the wikiarrow_forward
warning

Know the command first

aShell You runs real commands with real permissions. A wiped partition stays wiped. Basic knowledge of ADB and Linux commands is expected.
